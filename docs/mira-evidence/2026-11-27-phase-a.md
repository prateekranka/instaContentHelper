## What changed

- "Make this one" opens **Your words**. The words are not approved until she taps **Sounds like me**. Before this change, `makeTodayCard` approved the words and opened filming at once.
- **Make it simpler** and **Change it** each make one `reviseScript` call, only on a tap. The new version shows as "Mira's new version" with **Use this** / **Keep mine**. "Use this" adds a new script revision. The old words stay saved.
- Owner choice 1A: when changed words are approved, one `reviseStoryboard` call updates the shot words. Filming opens after that call. No server change and no deploy.
- New shots from that update are marked "changed, check again" only after filming has started. Before this fix, every shot showed "This shot changed" before she filmed anything.
- The app reopens on Your words when she leaves it there. Saved projects that were filming before Phase A keep their approval and progress.
- **Finish** after the last filmed shot opens **Done**: "N clips filmed", the caption with **Copy caption**, the clip order with check marks, and **Open Instagram** (opens the app, or the App Store page if Instagram is not installed). Mira does not post.
- Plain words from review item 9. "Read constraints from my request" is removed; the constraints stay editable by hand. The filming title is "Say your opening line" / "Say the next part" / "Say your last line".
