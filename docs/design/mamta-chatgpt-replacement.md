# Can ContentHelper replace Mamta's ChatGPT project?

Date: 2026-10-21. Source: the text of the 57 chats in the "Mamta fitness insta" ChatGPT project (3 September to mid-October 2026), compared with the app source. The project's knowledge files were not used.

## Short answer

No, not yet. The app does one job well: plan a reel before filming. In ChatGPT, Mamta mostly does the opposite. She already has a photo or video, then she asks for words, music, or a fix. About 5 of her 57 chats match what the app does today. About 25 more would match if the app had one new feature: **start from a photo or video she already has**.

## How Mamta really uses ChatGPT

### She starts with a photo, video, or screenshot

Most chats open with an attachment and one to five words:

- "Caption", "Song", "More", "Reply", "Review", "Improve the quality".
- She sends screenshots of brand DMs, follower comments, and stories with one word: "Reply".

She almost never starts with a blank plan. Her content comes from her life: races, trips, family, brand products.

### She corrects the tone with short follow-ups

This is her most repeated struggle. In 8 chats she pushed back because the text sounded fake, too long, or too proud:

- "Like me not ai generated"
- "Emotional.. like me not ai"
- "More like me", "Little more personal"
- "This sounds little too much"
- "It should not sound boastful"
- "Shorter", "More affectionate"

Each time, the next answer was shorter, warmer, simpler, and less polished. That is her voice.

### She mixes languages and types fast

- English, Hinglish, Hindi, and Punjabi all appear.
- Many messages are voice-typed, with typos ("Hive a few more options", "I fo t want story line").
- One chat is a Hindi voice note that failed to record.
- She wants options, then picks one ("More", "Give me a few more options for the start hook").

### She works under time pressure

Many chats happen the day before a race, at the race, or minutes before posting. She needs an answer she can use right away.

### She learns by seeing

"Use pro intelligence. Show me storyboards. I am a visual learner."

## Her struggles

1. **AI text does not sound like her.** She has to ask for "like me" again and again.
2. **AI changes her face** when it improves a photo. She repeats "do not change the face".
3. **Instagram labels her post as AI** after ChatGPT edits a photo. ChatGPT's own advice: put text on the untouched original photo in a normal editor.
4. **Her clips are low quality** (512×910, often forwarded). She wants them cleaned up before posting.
5. **Picking a song takes long**, and paid posts need music that is safe for ads.
6. **Brand deals have rules.** Required shots, #Ad, usage rights, rates, deadlines. She needs reminders and ready replies.
7. **She needs it now.** Race eve, race day, at the airport.

## Ease of use for a 61-year-old

What her chats show she needs:

- **Attach first, then tap.** She sends a photo, then one word. The app should take a photo or video first, then offer buttons: Caption, Song, Reply, Text on photo.
- **Buttons instead of typing.** Her follow-ups are always the same few words. Make them buttons: More, Shorter, More like me, More emotional, Less proud, In Hindi.
- **Voice input everywhere.** She voice-types in several languages.
- **Three to five options with one marked "My pick".** ChatGPT does this, and she uses it.
- **Pictures, not tables,** for plans.

What gets in her way in the app today (from the app source):

1. Getting a post takes about 5 steps through Plan. In ChatGPT it takes one attachment and one word.
2. App words she will not know: "Shoot Folio", "Light edit", "Unpublish", "Overwrite", "Make ready for Today", "Undo posted", "Creator voice", "References", "Source reference media", "Storyboard card preview", "Voiceover (with b-roll)", "Text-led videos".
3. 18 places use small fixed text (10 to 13 points) that does not grow with the phone's text size.
4. Some actions hide in "..." menus.

## Plan

The biggest gain is one new feature. It covers about 25 of her 57 chats.

| # | Change | Covers | Size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **"Help with this"** on Today. She adds a photo, video, or screenshot, then taps what she needs: Caption, Story text, Reply, or Song. She gets 3 to 5 options with "My pick" marked, and a Copy button. | Captions, replies, songs (about 25 chats) | Medium |
| 2 | **Tone buttons** on every piece of text: More, Shorter, More like me, More emotional, Less proud, In Hindi. Each makes a new version and keeps the old one. | "Like me" rewrites (8 chats) | Small to medium |
| 3 | **Learn her voice from her edits.** When she picks or changes a version, save it as an example of her voice for later answers. | Stops the "like me" loop | Medium |
| 4 | **Text on her original photo, made on the phone.** No AI touches the photo, so her face stays the same and Instagram does not add an AI label. Covers story text, covers, and simple carousels. | Overlays, carousels, covers (8 chats) | Medium |
| 5 | **Brand helper.** She adds a brief, contract, or DM screenshot. She gets the required shots and dates, a reply with her rate, a #Ad reminder, and music that is safe for ads. | Brand work (12 chats) | Medium |
| 6 | **Plain words and bigger text** in the existing screens (rename the words listed above, make all text grow, move actions out of "..." menus). | Ease of use | Small |
| 7 | **Storyboards up front** for planned reels, because she is a visual learner. | Planning (5 chats) | Small |
| Later | Photo and video clean-up, edit review, trending songs from live data. These need media processing or live data. Until then, the app can say what to fix and why. | 15 chats | Large |
| Never | Travel, health, money, and general questions. ChatGPT stays better for these. | 16 chats | None |

## How to tell it is done

- Mamta can take a photo from her camera roll and get a caption she likes in two taps or fewer.
- She asks for "like me" less often, because the first answer already sounds like her.
- No photo she posts from the app gets an Instagram AI label.
- Best check: watch Mamta use the app for one real post, one brand reply, and one follower reply, without help.

## Limits of this review

- 19 of the 57 chats were only partly readable. Older messages could not be scrolled into view.
- Most images and videos inside the chats could not be seen. Only their names and the replies about them were read.
- A few travel chats were asked by family members on her account.
